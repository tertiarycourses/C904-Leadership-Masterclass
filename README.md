## About the course

Leadership Masterclass helps learners lead with clarity, make reasoned decisions and support team performance. The six-topic teaching spine carries forward the original C904 deck, with additional visual frameworks, worked examples and twelve practical activity packs. The concept library supports further reading; the Lesson Plan identifies the live teaching route.

## Learning outcomes

- LO1: Explain leadership and management and choose a behaviour suited to the situation.
- LO2: Use active listening and specific feedback to build a shared understanding.
- LO3: Compare options using evidence, uncertainty and ethical constraints.
- LO4: Create meaningful goals and coach someone toward better performance.
- LO5: Resolve a work disagreement by exploring interests and fair options.
- LO6: Design an inclusive team charter, delegation agreement and personal development plan.

## Topics covered

1. **Leadership Foundations** — Self-awareness, emotional regulation, integrity, resilience and change.
2. **Communication and Collaboration** — Active listening, clear messages, feedback and cross-team working.
3. **Decision Making and Problem Solving** — Evidence, options, risk, bias and ethical judgement.
4. **Motivation and Performance** — Motivation theories, goals, coaching and fair performance support.
5. **Conflict Resolution and Negotiation** — Conflict patterns, interests, options and emotional steadiness.
6. **Team Building and Management** — Team roles, inclusion, delegation, trust and accountability.
