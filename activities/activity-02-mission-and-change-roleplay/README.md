# Activity 02: Mission and Change Roleplay

Leadership Masterclass · C904 · v4.0 · 35 minutes

## Goal

In this activity, you will select a style and create shared purpose using a fictional Meridian Services situation. You will discuss different perspectives, practise a leadership response and produce a reusable work artifact. The exercise connects the topic concepts to an everyday team decision, and the debrief focuses on how your response affects other people.

## What you will build

A completed mission and change roleplay worksheet, a peer observation checklist and a practical next action.

## Preparation

Read scenario.md. Open worksheet.docx or print worksheet.pdf. Work in pairs or triads; appoint an observer for roleplay. All organisations, people and numbers in these practice packs are fictional. No paid diagnostic tool is required.

## Steps

1. Identify what is changing and what each role fears losing. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

2. Draft a mission statement that describes a shared outcome in one sentence. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

3. Choose directive, participative, coaching or delegating behaviour for each role and justify the choice. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

4. Run a five-minute meeting with a leader, a sceptical specialist and a new colleague. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

5. Agree a limited experiment, an owner and a review date; debrief what improved commitment. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

## Test it

Check each item in checklist.md, then ask a peer to identify one clear strength and one concrete improvement. Confirm that your proposed action has an owner and a review point.

## Debrief

Which evidence changed your view? What trade-off remains? Which behaviour will you practise at work? Compare sample-completed-worksheet.pdf after your first attempt; it illustrates one possible response.
