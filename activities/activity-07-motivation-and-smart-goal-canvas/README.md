# Activity 07: Motivation and SMART Goal Canvas

Leadership Masterclass · C904 · v4.0 · 35 minutes

## Goal

In this activity, you will diagnose barriers and make a useful goal using a fictional Meridian Services situation. You will discuss different perspectives, practise a leadership response and produce a reusable work artifact. The exercise connects the topic concepts to an everyday team decision, and the debrief focuses on how your response affects other people.

## What you will build

A completed motivation and smart goal canvas worksheet, a peer observation checklist and a practical next action.

## Preparation

Read scenario.md. Open worksheet.docx or print worksheet.pdf. Work in pairs or triads; appoint an observer for roleplay. All organisations, people and numbers in these practice packs are fictional. No paid diagnostic tool is required.

## Steps

1. Check whether the issue involves clarity, resources, capability, workload or motivation. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

2. Use intrinsic/extrinsic and autonomy/capability/connection lenses to form questions, not labels. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

3. Write a SMART goal with baseline, target, owner and time window. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

4. Choose a support action and a balancing measure that reveals transferred workload. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

5. Agree a weekly review and explain how you would revise support if results do not improve. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

## Test it

Check each item in checklist.md, then ask a peer to identify one clear strength and one concrete improvement. Confirm that your proposed action has an owner and a review point.

## Debrief

Which evidence changed your view? What trade-off remains? Which behaviour will you practise at work? Compare sample-completed-worksheet.pdf after your first attempt; it illustrates one possible response.
