# Activity 12: 30-Day Leadership Development Plan

Leadership Masterclass · C904 · v4.0 · 35 minutes

## Goal

In this activity, you will convert learning into a measurable habit using a fictional Meridian Services situation. You will discuss different perspectives, practise a leadership response and produce a reusable work artifact. The exercise connects the topic concepts to an everyday team decision, and the debrief focuses on how your response affects other people.

## What you will build

A completed 30-day leadership development plan worksheet, a peer observation checklist and a practical next action.

## Preparation

Read scenario.md. Open worksheet.docx or print worksheet.pdf. Work in pairs or triads; appoint an observer for roleplay. All organisations, people and numbers in these practice packs are fictional. No paid diagnostic tool is required.

## Steps

1. Select one observable behaviour linked to a course learning outcome. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

2. Choose a real practice opportunity and a feedback partner who experiences the behaviour. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

3. Write an if–then plan for the trigger most likely to disrupt the habit. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

4. Define evidence, a baseline if available, and reviews on days 7, 14 and 30. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

5. Explain the plan to a partner, revise vague wording and keep a copy for workplace use. Write your reasoning in the worksheet; compare it with your partner before moving on.

## Test it

Check each item in checklist.md, then ask a peer to identify one clear strength and one concrete improvement. Confirm that your proposed action has an owner and a review point.

## Debrief

Which evidence changed your view? What trade-off remains? Which behaviour will you practise at work? Compare sample-completed-worksheet.pdf after your first attempt; it illustrates one possible response.
